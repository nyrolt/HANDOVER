System Capabilities

Engineered for academic faculty and department workflows

Every workflow required to coordinate courses, calculate grades, and review student projects without switching between disparate tools.

📊

Excel-Like Continuous Marks Gradebook

Navigate seamlessly using Tab, Shift+Tab, and Enter keys. Frozen student names and roll numbers ensure frictionless horizontal scrolling. Automatic bounding clamps prevent out-of-bounds inputs (strictly 0 to 20).

🏫

Independent Class Sections

Strict multi-tenancy ensures CSE-A, CSE-B, and IT-A records never leak. Each section maintains isolated roll rosters, coursework, and teams.

Dual Group Project Engine

📥

Bi-Directional CSV Synchronization

Export complete section records to clean CSV formats ready for registrar submission. Seamlessly batch-import student rosters from university administrative files.

Why Excel Spreadsheets Fail Academic Departments

Desktop spreadsheets are calculators. Handover is an institutional operating system.

The Fragile Old Way

Manual Excel Spreadsheets

  • ❌ Version Drift & Loss: Scores of marks_final_v2_edit.xlsx scattered across USB sticks and emails.
  • ❌ Bad Bounds & Errors: Accidental 25/20 scores entered, broken formulas, and #REF! errors.
  • ❌ Zero Privacy: Printing marks on bulletin boards or sending bulk spreadsheets revealing peer scores.
  • ❌ Chaotic Teams: Uncoordinated WhatsApp group chats and lost handwritten paper proposals.
The Modern Standard

Handover Academic OS

  • ✓ Single Atomic Source of Truth: Live persistent database with real-time continuous updates.
  • ✓ Strict Bounds & Instant Grading: Automated clamping (0 - 20) and letter grades (O, A+, A, B+, B, F).
  • ✓ Role-Isolated Portals: Students access only their personal verified scorecard with complete privacy.
  • ✓ Structured Proposals: Online teammate invites; faculty review, approve, and guide in one click.
